THRONEFALL

Build by day. Ride out by night. Hold the keep until dawn.
A from-scratch browser recreation in three.js.

WASD / arrows ride the king · Space ability, or build when you are standing on a plot · Left-click a plot to build or upgrade from anywhere · Mouse wheel zoom · Enter start the night · Esc menu.
Left Ctrl gathers your soldiers and they follow; hold it and they post up instead. F and 1–4 choose which of them the order takes. Left Alt locks your attack onto one enemy.

Gold arrives each morning from houses and farms. Spend it on walls, towers and garrisons. Enemies march from the marked spawn points toward your keep — if the keep falls, the run is over. You can die: the king revives at the keep after a few seconds.
