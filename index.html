Built with Claude Code · no game engine

Five games, no engine

Five commercial games rebuilt from scratch as browser games — plain ES modules and three.js, no engine, no build step, no backend. Every sprite, mesh and sound is generated in code: there is not a single image or audio file in any of them. Click one and play; nothing to install.

Tiny Wings

One-button momentum

Slide a round bird down procedurally generated hills, timing each dive to launch off the crest. Night is chasing you, and every island buys more daylight. Includes a race against three AI rivals.

Hold mouse or Space to dive · release to glide

Play

Slither.io

Arena survival

Grow by eating glowing orbs among 30 AI snakes. Boost costs mass and sheds it behind you. Your own body is harmless — but any head touching any body dies, so the smallest snake can kill the biggest.

Mouse to steer · hold click or Space to boost

Play

Motherload

Mining · resource management

Fly a mining pod underground, drill for ore, and haul it back up to sell before the fuel runs out. Deeper means richer minerals and lava, gas pockets and undrillable rock. Six upgrade lines and a boss at the bottom.

Arrows / WASD thrust and drill · E vendor · I inventory

Play

Thronefall

Day-build / night-defend strategy

Build a holding by day with no time pressure, then ride out as a mounted king and defend it through the night. Ten maps, 17 buildings with branching upgrades, 21 enemy types plus bosses, perks and mutators.

WASD to ride · click a glowing plot to build · Space ability

Play

Merge Dragons

Merge puzzle

Drag three or more matching objects together to merge them up a tier — five at once pays double. Merging life flowers heals the grey dead land, revealing more board, while dragons wander and harvest on their own.

Drag matching objects together · tap to harvest

Play

Best in a desktop browser. All five need WebGL or Canvas and a keyboard or mouse, so phones will struggle — Thronefall and Motherload in particular expect keys. Progress saves to your own browser's local storage, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Each game folder carries its own README.md and a FIDELITY.md listing honestly which features of the original are done, partial, or missing.

Built as a benchmark: five games in one three-hour session, 33,006 lines across 97 modules, one vendored dependency (three.js r169, MIT).
